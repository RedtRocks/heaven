# Keepsake

A personal memory archive the owner builds about their own life, which can later power a filtered, first-person clone that chosen people can talk to.

## People

**Owner**:
The person whose life the archive is about. The only person who can add to it or create a clone of it.
_Avoid_: User (ambiguous), subject

**Visitor**:
Anyone other than the Owner who talks to the Clone, such as family or friends.
_Avoid_: Family member, guest, user

**Executor**:
A person the Owner names who can switch the Clone into Legacy Mode after the Owner's death.
_Avoid_: Admin, trustee

## Archive

**Entry**:
Raw input the Owner gives, such as a written or spoken summary of their day. Later this may also come from a connected device.
_Avoid_: Log, note, upload

**Memory**:
A single event, fact, opinion or story taken from one or more Entries. It covers one event only, so that sharing one Memory never reveals what happened next to it. It is the unit that gets searched, filtered and cited.
_Avoid_: Record, item

**Sealed**:
A mark on a Memory that keeps it from ever reaching the Clone, whoever the Participants are. It overrides every other Visibility rule. Meant for secrets. Memories in sensitive categories (health, money, romance, other people's secrets) are Sealed automatically until the Owner unseals them.
_Avoid_: Locked, hidden, super-private

**Release Digest**:
The message sent to the Owner before a Holding Period ends. It lists the Memories about to reach the Clone and the Visibility proposed for each one. If the Owner doesn't reply, the proposals take effect.
_Avoid_: Review email, notification

**Style Sample**:
Something the Owner said or wrote in real conversation (their side of a chat export, a voice note they sent), used only to learn how they talk. It is never a Memory and never quoted as fact. Other people's words are dropped.
_Avoid_: Training data, chat import

**Holding Period**:
The delay (e.g. a week or a month) between a Memory being saved and it becoming available to the Clone. During this time the Owner can review, change or seal it.
_Avoid_: Cooldown, embargo

**Participant**:
A person who took part in, or is the subject of, a Memory. A Visitor who is a Participant in a Memory may be allowed to see it even though it is otherwise Private.
_Avoid_: Tagged person, mention

**Visibility**:
Who, other than the Owner, a Memory can reach through the Clone. The default is Private.
_Avoid_: Privacy level, permission

**Trait**:
A standing fact about the Owner (a preference, habit or opinion), either stated directly or inferred from patterns across many Memories, e.g. "favourite place for pizza is Domino's".
_Avoid_: Profile field, attribute

**Check-in Question**:
A question the Owner is asked on a regular schedule so the archive covers things daily summaries rarely mention (favourites, beliefs, advice).
_Avoid_: Survey, prompt

**Seed Interview**:
A one-time batch of Check-in Questions the Owner answers at the start, so the Clone has Traits and a personality before daily Entries build up.
_Avoid_: Onboarding, setup quiz

**Relationship**:
The Owner's setup for one Visitor: how the Clone should behave toward them, captured through a guided set of questions, plus what they are allowed to see.
_Avoid_: Contact, access tier

## Conversation

**Memory Assistant**:
The Owner talking to their own full archive. It addresses the Owner in the second person ("you said…").
_Avoid_: Clone, bot

**Clone**:
A first-person persona of the Owner that Visitors talk to. It sees only a filtered subset of the archive, answers mainly from Memories, and may reason loosely on generic questions without inventing facts about the Owner's life.
_Avoid_: Avatar, AI version, twin

**Likeness**:
The Owner's cloned voice and face, made only from recordings the Owner provides. The Clone speaks and appears through it.
_Avoid_: Deepfake, avatar

**Legacy Mode**:
The Clone's state after the Executor confirms the Owner has died. Before that, the Clone is already available to Visitors. Legacy Mode is only a switch for after-death rules.
_Avoid_: Death mode, memorial mode
